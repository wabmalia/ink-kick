      How to play

      WASD or ↑←↓→
      Run. You turn slower with the ball.
      Shift
      Sprint until your stamina runs out. Drops your defensive stance.
      Spacehold
      Kick. Hold to power up, let go to strike. A quick pass to a teammate you're facing curls onto their feet. Near goal, a shot that a curl couldn't save turns slowly until one could. Hold a direction just after to curl it.
      Space+Shift
      Fake. Tap sprint mid-charge to call it off.
      Space
      Tackle. Pokes up close, slides from further or behind.
      X
      Same as Space.
      HEsc
      This help. Esc also leaves solo.
      Run. Put your thumb down anywhere on the left: that's the stick's centre. You turn slower with the ball.
      Sprint
      Sprint until your stamina runs out. Drops your defensive stance.
      Kickhold
      Kick. Hold to power up, let go to strike. A quick pass to a teammate you're facing curls onto their feet. Steer just after to curl it.
      Kick→Sprint
      Fake. Mid-charge, flick your thumb from Kick onto Sprint to call it off and sprint away.
      Kick
      Tackle. Pokes up close, slides from further or behind.

      Attributes

      Every player starts with 5 in each. Take points off one to put them on another, between 1 and 10.

      • Running speed
      • Acceleration
      • Tires quicker
      • Heavier touch sprinting
      • Sprint and tackle longer
      • Getting your breath back
      • Turning with the ball
      • Speed on the ball
      • Quick stops and cuts
      • Shot power
      • Charging a kick
      • Curl
      • Poke and slide reach
      • Beating strong players
      • Less breath per tackle
      • Riding out tackles
      • Getting up after a slide
      • Shoved less in a slide
      • Less breath lost if robbed

      No build is best: every strength has something that counters it. Points left unspent do nothing.

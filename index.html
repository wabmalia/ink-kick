      How to play

      WASD
      • WASD or arrows to move.
      • Hold a direction right after a kick to curl it.
      Shift
      • Hold to sprint, while stamina lasts.
      Space
      • With the ball: hold to charge, let go to shoot or pass.
      • Without it: tap to poke, hold to slide.
      • Tap Shift mid-charge to fake. Space+Shift
      • X works like Space.

      H this help · Esc also leaves solo

      • Drag the stick to move.
      • Steer right after a kick to curl it.
      Sprint
      • Hold to sprint, while stamina lasts.
      • Flick onto Kick to slide or start a kick. SprintKick
      Kick
      • With the ball: hold to charge, let go to shoot or pass.
      • Without it: tap to poke, hold to slide.
      • Flick onto Sprint mid-charge to fake. KickSprint

      Attributes

      Every player starts with 5 in each. Take points off one to put them on another, between 1 and 10.

      • Running speed

      • Getting your breath back

      • Speed on the ball
      • Quick stops and cuts
      • Shot power
      • Charging a kick
      • Curl
      • Poke and slide reach
      • Beating strong players
      • Less breath per tackle
      • Riding out tackles
      • Getting up after a slide
      • Shoved less in a slide
      • Less breath lost if robbed

      No build is best: every strength has something that counters it. Points left unspent do nothing.

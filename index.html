How to play

WASD or ↑←↓→
Run. With the ball at your feet you turn more slowly, so it swings round you.
Shift
Sprint until your stamina runs dry, then you tire for a moment. Stamina shows left of the ring under you.
Spacehold
With the ball: kick. Hold to power it up, release to strike. Short is a pass, long is a shot. Power shows right of the ring.
Space+Shift
Fake. Tap sprint while charging and the kick is called off, the ball kept.
Spacetap / hold
Without the ball: tackle. Tap to poke a foot in, hold to slide. Slide straight into the ball to keep it. Nobody can poke the ball off your back.
X
Works the same as Space.
HEsc
Open and close this. Esc also leaves a solo game. Solo pauses while this is open.

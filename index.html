How to play

WASD or ↑←↓→
Run. You turn slower with the ball.
Shift
Sprint until your stamina runs out.
Spacehold
Kick. Hold to power up (you can't turn meanwhile), let go to strike. Hold a direction just after to curl it.
Space+Shift
Fake. Tap sprint mid-charge to call it off.
Space
Tackle. Pokes up close, slides from further or behind.
X
Same as Space.
HEsc
This help. Esc also leaves solo.

Attributes

Every player starts with 5 in each. Take points off one to put them on another, between 1 and 10.

Pace
  • Running speed
  • Acceleration
  • Tires quicker
  • Heavier touch sprinting
Stamina
  • Sprint and tackle longer
  • Getting your breath back
Dribbling
  • Turning with the ball
  • Speed on the ball
  • Quick stops and cuts
Power
  • Shot power
  • Charging a kick
  • Curl
Tackling
  • Poke and slide reach
  • Beating strong players
  • Less breath per tackle
Strength
  • Riding out tackles
  • Getting up after a slide
  • Shoved less in a slide
  • Less breath lost if robbed

No build is best: every strength has something that counters it. Points left unspent do nothing.
